Privacy

What this site keeps about you

Short version: almost nothing. No cookies, no tracking, no ads, and nothing is sold.

Version 1 · 26 November 2026

Who runs this site

This site is run by Aúpa, a photographer, as a private person. For anything on this page, write to the email address.

When you visit

  • No cookies, no tracking. The site does not count you, follow you or build a profile of you.
  • One thing is remembered in your own browser: whether you chose the light or the dark look. It stays on your device.
  • Fonts and photos come from this site, not from other companies.
  • Hosting. Like every website, the company that hosts it (GitHub, for now) sees your IP address and browser details to deliver the pages and keep the site safe. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Aúpa's letter

  • If you sign up, your email address is stored by Kit, the service that sends the letter. You confirm by clicking a link in an email first.
  • It is used only to send you the letter, twice a month. It is never sold or shared.
  • Every letter has an unsubscribe link. One click and you're off the list.
  • Kit's own rules: Kit privacy policy.

When you write to me or book a walk

If you email me or message me on WhatsApp, I keep your message and contact details only as long as I need them to reply, to run your walk, or to send you a print. Then I delete them.

If you're in one of my photos

I photograph streets, trains and people. If you see yourself here and want the photo taken down, write to me and I will remove it.

Your rights

You can ask what I have about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it. Write to the email address and I'll answer within one month.

Changes

If this page changes, the version number and date at the top change too.
