02 — Photo Walks

Kolkata, honestly.

Join a walk Walk stories

Not a tour. A day out with someone who lives here.

I'm 20, I'm a photographer, and I'll show you the Kolkata most people actually live in — not the one from the back of an Uber. We take the bus. We take the ferry. We eat where people eat. I'll tell you a bit of the history of every place we stop, show you where to point your camera, and tell you honestly where to be careful.

Bring any camera — a phone is fine. By the end you'll have photos you're proud of, and you'll have seen the city properly.

The walks

What you get

  • Me as your guide for the whole walk
  • Local transport the way locals use it — bus, tram, ferry, local train
  • The history and stories behind each stop
  • Photography tips as we go — composition, light, how to photograph people respectfully
  • Honest safety advice: where to watch your bag, what to avoid, what's fine
  • Photos of you on the walk, sent afterwards
  • Chai stop included

What I don't do

  • No Ubers, no AC coaches, no hotel pickups — we meet at a bus stop or station
  • No "poverty tourism" — we photograph people with respect, and we ask
  • No staged or polished version of the city
  • Meals beyond chai, transport fares and entry tickets are paid by you (they're cheap — I'll tell you what to carry)
  • Not a camera workshop with rented gear — bring your own

How it works

  1. Message me with the walk, date and how many people.
  2. I confirm on WhatsApp and send the meeting point and what to bring.
  3. We walk. Groups are small — up to 6 people.
  4. A few days later I send the photos I took of you. If one of yours or mine makes it into an exhibition, I'll ask you first.

Questions

Is it safe?

Yes — these are areas people live and work in every day. I'll tell you honestly where to keep your phone in your pocket and how to dress. If something isn't a good idea, I'll say so.

Do I need a proper camera?

No. A phone is completely fine. If you have a camera, bring it and I'll help you with settings.

How much walking is there?

A fair bit, plus getting on and off buses. Wear comfortable shoes. Tell me beforehand if you need it slower — we'll adjust.

Can I book a private walk?

Yes — just you, your friends or family. Message me for a price.

What if it rains?

Monsoon Kolkata is beautiful to photograph. If it's really bad we reschedule, free.
