Hi, I'm Your Name.

I'm 20, I grew up between Kolkata and Shantiniketan, and I don't go anywhere without a camera.

I shoot mostly on the street — people, light, the in-between moments. Some of it ends up on gallery walls, some of it in prints people take home, and a lot of it comes from walking the city with strangers who become friends by the end of the day.

I'm honest about what I show you. No filters on the city, no Uber bubble. Just the real thing.
